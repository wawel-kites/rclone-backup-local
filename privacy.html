Rclone Backup Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Access and use

After the Google account owner authorizes it, rclone uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage backups. This permission limits access to files created by the app or specifically authorized for it.

To create, verify, and restore backups, rclone processes file contents and metadata such as names, sizes, and modification times. Backup files are transferred between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Google user data is used only for backup and restoration.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's computer. Backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive and kept according to the owner's retention settings. This website does not receive backup contents or OAuth credentials.

Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with other recipients. Communication with Google Drive is required for the backup to work.

Revoking access and deleting backups

The owner can revoke the app's permission in Google Account connections. Backups already stored in Drive can be removed in Google Drive.

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
